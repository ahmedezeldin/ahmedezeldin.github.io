Project

Medicine Cooler

Team engineering design project · University of Lethbridge (ENGG 1600)

A four-person first-year design project: a smart cooler to keep temperature-sensitive medication such as insulin within a 2–8°C target range. I wrote the ESP32 firmware and designed the enclosure.

  • Wrote the ESP32 firmware (Arduino/C++): DS18B20 temperature sensing, live readout on a 128×64 OLED, threshold-based relay switching for the cooling stage, and an escalating buzzer alert.
  • Designed the full enclosure — seven 3D-printed parts (insulated outer shell, inner liner, electronics bay, and lids) plus the insulation layout.
  • Assembled the Peltier thermoelectric cooling stage and integrated it with the relay control, then bench-tested the full system with an ice-water setup.
